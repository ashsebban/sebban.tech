Rules of the raid

  • Shinobi move first and see the whole board. Capture the Shogun with either assassin within 20 Shinobi moves.
  • Samurai win by catching both assassins or keeping the Shogun safe until dawn. Enemy pieces are visible to Samurai only in lantern light or where a blind move ran into them.
  • Assassins can slip diagonally over one piece or wall to an empty square. Capturing the Shogun takes a clear diagonal.
  • Shinobi towers can grapple in a straight line over one wall to a free square. Samurai lantern towers light the eight neighboring squares. Slips and grapples start and land outside lantern light.
  • Patrols can make normal knight jumps or capture one square straight ahead, behind, left, or right. Samurai pawns and patrols may try a blind strike. A miss or a blocked move uses their turn.
  • Walls block sliding pieces. Samurai can spend a turn shifting a wall one empty square within ranks 3 and 4.
  • Other moves follow standard chess. Pawns promote to queens. There is no check or castling.
