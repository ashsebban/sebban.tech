How to play

  • White moves first from the ordinary chess opening. Black marks one pawn and draws a private target card: the king, queen, or one specific starting bishop.
  • Once the spy reaches White’s half and survives White’s reply, it can spend a turn taking its target one square in any direction or a knight’s jump away. Select the spy, then the target, to ambush.
  • Capture the spy and both pieces fall, including en passant. If the disappearing capturer leaves its king exposed, Black wins. Black wins if the capturer is the target or king, or if the explosion leaves the White king exposed.
  • Once per game, select a White bishop, choose Inspect a pawn, then tap a black pawn on its open diagonal. Only White sees the answer. White still makes a normal move.
  • Checkmate also wins. If another piece takes the target, or the spy promotes, its mission ends and ordinary chess continues.
