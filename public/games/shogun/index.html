How to play

  • Shinobi move first. Land an assassin bishop beside the sleeping Shogun to win immediately. Only assassin bishops can hide.
  • The courtyard (ranks 1–2) stays lit. Wall guards watch one square forward. A tower watches straight ahead until the first piece. Select a tower and an adjacent square to turn its lantern for one move.
  • Samurai pawns on rank 2 form walls. Horizontal or diagonal chains reaching rank 2 stay fortified through rank 4.
  • Move a grappling rook directly outside a wall, then select the wall to attach a rope. On a later turn, select a neighboring raider and the empty square beyond the wall to climb. Moving or capturing the crew releases the rope.
  • Landing beside the sleeping Shogun wins before detection. Otherwise, spotting or capturing an assassin, or checking the Shogun, rings the alarm. Crossing a watched square is safe if you land unseen.
  • At the alarm both assassins become visible, the Shogun wakes, and cavalry deploy on b1 and f1 if empty. White acts next. Win by checkmate, or as White by capturing both assassin bishops.
