How to play

  • Take both castles. A castle stays put and can fall to a direct capture or a clear arrow shot.
  • Shoot, then jump. A horse archer can optionally shoot one enemy piece or castle exactly two squares away in a row or column, with an empty square in between. It must then make a knight jump, capturing any enemy on its landing square. That can take two enemies in one turn. An archer can also jump without shooting.
  • Rally with your king. Select your king and tap any adjacent friendly piece to swap places for your turn. Castles stay fixed. Pawns on their own back rank still move one square forward when clear.
  • Other pieces. Queens, bishops, kings, and pawns move as in chess. Pawns promote to queens. Kings can be captured. The game has no check or castling.
  • Draws. A side with no move passes. Threefold repetition, both sides unable to move, or 60 turns without a pawn move or capture is a draw.
